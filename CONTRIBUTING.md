# Contributing

## Principles

- Keep guidance framework-agnostic and language-agnostic.
- Prefer concise rules with clear reasons.
- Put reusable rules in `standards/`; put procedures in `skills/`; put fillable artifacts in `templates/`.
- Avoid vague filler, speculative advice, and duplicated sections.

## Skill Changes

Every skill must use the standard front matter and headings exactly. Keep examples generic and focused on engineering decisions.

## Review Checklist

- The change improves production usefulness.
- The guidance is internally consistent.
- Related skills and standards are cross-referenced.
- Markdown renders cleanly.
- No framework-specific assumptions were introduced.
